Heavy WebGL belongs behind a launcher and an error boundary. The shell stays boring on purpose so failures do not take the whole brand down.

Use `next/dynamic` with `ssr: false`, measure bundle deltas, and give visitors a plain-language reason when their GPU or camera cannot play along.
