## The pitch

When the site is the resume, you want fast loads, cheap hosting, and a repo auditors can read.

## What we optimize for

Editorial content in Git, validated at build time, keeps marketing and demos aligned without a CMS bill.

## Tradeoff

You plan content migrations like code reviews. For a solo portfolio, that is usually a feature.
