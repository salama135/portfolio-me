Home Farm

Wold

A real Cotswold valley near Chadlington. Home Farm is yours: a dozen fields, 24 sheep, one tractor and £15,000.

Do the work yourself or pay someone, sell what you grow, look after the animals, keep the neighbours on side, and find new ways to make the place pay. Then automate it. Time never stops.

Phone: drag the left side to move, the right side to look. Buttons appear for vehicles and tools.
Keyboard: WASD, drag to look, E to get in or do things, F to lower a tool, G to change tool, P for the Farm panel, M for the map.
Farm (top right) is where you run the business. The number beside it sets how fast time passes.
